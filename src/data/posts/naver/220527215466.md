							<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic;color:rgb(0, 0, 0)">우리가 알고 있는 자유형은 사실은 크롤(crawl)영이라는 영법이다. 자유형은 'free'라는 단어에서 알 수 있듯이 크롤영 외에도 다양한 영법을 사용할 수 있다. 이런 '자유'형 경기에서 어떠한 영법들을 사용할 수 있는지, 그리고 스타트는 어떤 방식으로 할 수 있는지 알아보자.</span></p><p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic"><br /></span></p><p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic;color:rgb(0, 0, 0)">먼저, FINA에서 규정하고 있는 '자유형'의 개념과 '스타트' 규칙은 다음과 같다.</span></p><p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic"><br /></span></p><p><br /></p><p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic;color:rgb(0, 0, 0)"><b>자유형</b></span></p><p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic"><br /></span></p><p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic;color:rgb(0, 0, 0)">SW 5.1 Freestyle means that in an event so designated the swimmer may swim any style, except that in individual medley or medley relay events, freestyle means any style other than backstroke, breaststroke or butterfly.</span></p><p><span style="line-height:1.5;font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic;color:rgb(0, 0, 0)">SW 5.2 Some part of the swimmer must touch the wall upon completion of each length and at the finish.</span></p><p><span style="line-height:1.5;font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic;color:rgb(0, 0, 0)">SW 5.3 Some part of the swimmer must break the surface of the water throughout the race, except it shall be permissible for the swimmer to be completely submerged during the turn and for a distance of not more than 15 metres after the start and each turn. By that point, the head must have broken the surface.</span></p><p><span style="line-height:1.5"><br /></span></p><p><span style="line-height:1.5;font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic;color:rgb(0, 0, 0)">SW 5.1 '자유형'이라 함은 '자유형'이라고 지칭된 경기에서는 어떤 영법도 가능하나 '개인혼영 및 혼계영' 경기에서는 접영/배영/평영을 제외한 어떤 영법도 가능함을 의미한다.</span></p><p><span style="line-height:1.5;font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic;color:rgb(0, 0, 0)">SW 5.2 경기 중 및 경기 종료 시 다다르게 되는 레인의 끝 부분은 반드시 터치되어야 한다.</span></p><p><span style="line-height:1.5;font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic;color:rgb(0, 0, 0)">SW 5.3 신체의 일부는 항상 물 밖으로 나와 있어야 한다. 단, 스타트 및 턴 직후 15미터 지점까지는 잠영이 허용되며, 이 지점 이전에 머리 부분이 물 밖으로 나와야 한다.</span></p><p><br /></p><p><br /></p><p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic;color:rgb(0, 0, 0)"><b>스타트</b></span></p><p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic"><br /></span></p><p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic;color:rgb(0, 0, 0)">SW 4.1 The start in Freestyle, Breaststroke, Butterfly and Individual Medley races shall be with a dive. On the long whistle (SW 2.1.5) from the referee the swimmers shall step onto the starting platform and remain there. On the starter's command "take your marks", they shall immediately take up a starting position with at least one foot at the front of the starting platforms. The position of the hands is not relevant. When all swimmers are stationary, the starter shall give the starting signal.</span></p><p><span style="line-height:1.5;font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic;color:rgb(0, 0, 0)">SW 4.2 The start in Backstroke and Medley Relay races shall be from the water. At the referee's first long whistle (SW 2.1.5), the swimmers shall immediately enter the water. At the Referee's second long whistle the swimmers shall return without undue delay to the starting position (SW 6.1). When all swimmers have assumed their starting positions, the starter shall give the command "take your marks". When all swimmers are stationary, the starter shall give the starting signal.</span></p><p><span style="line-height:1.5;font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic;color:rgb(0, 0, 0)">SW 4.3 In Olympic Games, World Championships and other FINA events the command "Take your marks" shall be in English and the start shall be by multiple loudspeakers, mounted one at each starting platform.</span></p><p><span style="line-height:1.5;font-size:11pt;font-family:나눔고딕, NanumGothic;color:rgb(0, 0, 0)">SW 4.4 Any swimmer starting before the starting signal has been given, shall be disqualified. If the starting signal sounds before the disqualification is declared, the race shall continue and the swimmer or swimmers shall be disqualified upon completion of the race. If the disqualification is declared before the starting signal, the signal shall not be given, but the remaining swimmers shall be called back and start again.
